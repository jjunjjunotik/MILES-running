## English

**App name**: NailSense: Nail Photo Notes

**Subtitle (App Store)**: Describe and track your nails

**Short description (Google Play)**: Describe how your nails look in a photo and keep track of changes over time.

**Promotional text (App Store)**: Take a nail photo and get a plain-language description of what it shows, with everyday care tips and a clear note on when to see a doctor.

**Keywords (App Store)**: nail,nails,nail care,manicure,nail photo,nail journal,cuticle,nail tracker,fingernail,toenail

**Description**:

NailSense describes how your nails look in a photo, in plain language, and helps you keep track of changes over time.

HOW IT WORKS
• Take or choose a close-up photo of one nail.
• NailSense describes what's visible: color, surface, shape and the skin around the nail.
• You get everyday care tips, and a clear note on signs that are worth showing a doctor.
• Save results to your history and compare them later.

WHAT NAILSENSE IS NOT
NailSense is for general information and reference. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or rule out any condition. A photo alone can't tell what's causing a change. If you're worried about your nails, or a change is new, spreading or painful, see a doctor or dermatologist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

PRIVACY
• Your photo is sent only to create the description and is not stored on our servers.
• Saved photos stay on your device.
• No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold or used to train AI models.
• Delete your history or your account at any time in the app.

PLANS
• Free: 3 scans a month with full results.
• Pro: up to 20 scans a day. Auto-renewing monthly or yearly subscription, with a free trial where offered.
Warning-sign checks and advice on when to see a doctor are included on every plan.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account (or Google account) at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in your account settings.

**설명**:

NailSense는 사진 속 손톱의 모습을 쉬운 말로 설명하고, 시간이 지나며 달라지는 모습을 기록하도록 돕는 앱이에요.

이렇게 써요
• 손톱 하나를 가까이 찍거나 사진을 골라요.
• 색, 표면, 모양, 손톱 주변 피부처럼 사진에 보이는 것을 설명해 드려요.

• 결과를 기록에 저장하고 나중에 비교해요.

NailSense가 아닌 것
NailSense는 일반적인 정보와 참고를 위한 앱이에요. 의료기기가 아니며 질병을 진단하거나 치료하지 않고, 질병이 없다고 판단하지도 않아요. 사진만으로는 변화의 원인을 알 수 없어요. 손톱이 걱정되거나 변화가 새로 생겼거나 번지거나 아프다면 의사나 피부과 전문의와 상담하세요. 응급 상황이면 119에 연락하세요.
